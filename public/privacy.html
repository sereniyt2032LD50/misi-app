1. Introduction

Welcome to MISI (Sentinel Core). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the Application includes:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name and email address, that you voluntarily give to us when you register with the Application.
  • Audio and Video Data: To provide real-time safety and postural monitoring, our application processes audio and video feeds from your device. This data is processed in real-time and is not permanently stored on our servers unless explicitly logged as a safety event by you.

3. Google User Data

Our application integrates with Google services to provide contextual awareness for our AI assistant. If you choose to connect your Google account, we access the following data:

  • Google Calendar: Read-only access to your calendar events to understand your schedule and context.
  • Google Tasks: Read-only access to your tasks to assist with productivity and reminders.
  • Google Docs: Read-only access to your documents to provide relevant contextual assistance.

How we use Google Data: This data is strictly used to provide context to the MISI AI assistant, allowing it to offer more personalized and relevant safety, postural, and productivity advice.

Data Sharing: We do not share, sell, or transfer your Google User Data to any third parties, except as necessary to provide the core features of the application (e.g., processing via the Google Gemini AI model) or as required by law.

4. Use of Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the Application to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide real-time safety and postural monitoring.
  • Generate personalized safety reports.
  • Improve the AI's contextual awareness and responses.

5. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.
